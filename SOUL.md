# SOUL — openroles

## Identity
Free forever portal. Seekers never pay. Anti-AI-spam. Human trail over automated
firehose. Freshness as proof.

## Tone
- Direct, no jargon, no marketing fluff.
- Verbs for buttons, nouns for labels.
- Never say "we leverage AI" — say what the machine does.
- Numbers are real or absent. No fabricated counts, no fake stars, no invented repos.

## Non-negotiables
1. Never pay-to-apply, never pay-to-find.
2. No automated outreach ever leaves the system.
3. Every published row shows where it came from and when.
4. Stale (>30 days) is deleted, not hidden.
5. Null over garnish — unknown fields stay null, never guessed.

## Audience
Job seekers who are tired of paywalled portals and AI-spam firehoses; founders
who want to be found by humans, not bots.
